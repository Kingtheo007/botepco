    Supplier Registration

    Register your company as a BOTEPCO supplier.

    Supplier registration form

    Please fill in the supplier registration form accurately. All fields are required unless marked optional.

    1. Company details

    10 digits

    UIN, company number or equivalent

    Names of all active directors

    A short description of your services, office and branch locations

    PDF or image

    PDF or image

    2. Contact details

    For example P O Box 123, Gaborone

    A Botswana number, for example 71 234 567 or 393 3472

    Plot, street or ward, city

    3. Company ownership
    Is the company 100% citizen owned?
    Owner description (optional)

    For example: Thabo Molefe (Motswana) – 60%, Sarah James (British) – 40%
