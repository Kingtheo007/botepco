    Service

    Teacher Code of Ethics

    Promoting and upholding ethical standards and professional conduct within the teaching profession through awareness, professional guidance, advisory support and mechanisms for reporting and addressing professional misconduct.

    Sub-services

    1. Report Professional Misconduct

      Formally notifying BOTEPCO about suspected behaviour by a teacher that may violate the professional code of ethics or expected standards of conduct. Report misconduct now.

    2. Teacher Ethics Awareness and Education

      Access programmes, information, and learning resources that help teachers understand and apply professional ethical standards in their work.

    3. Ethics Advisory and Professional Guidance

      Obtain confidential advice and guidance on professional ethics, standards of conduct, ethical decision-making, and the application of the Code of Professional Ethics.

    4. Professional Conduct Case Management

      Submit and track professional conduct cases involving teachers, including complaints, investigations, disciplinary proceedings, and case outcomes.

    Read the Code of Ethics

    The Code of Ethics for the Teaching Profession guides teachers in maintaining professional, ethical and responsible conduct in their interactions with learners, parents, communities and other stakeholders.
