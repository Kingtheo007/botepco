    Teacher Registration & Licensing

    What are the requirements for Teaching Registration & Licensing?
    • Certified copies of Teaching qualification
    • Certified copies of valid Identification document
    • Evaluation of Qualification Report from Botswana Qualification Authority (BQA)
    What is a Teaching License?

    A teaching license is a professional practice document issued by BOTEPCO. It formally recognizes an educator’s legitimacy to teach in Botswana. It verifies that the holder has met all required qualifications, professional standards and code of ethics.

    How long is the Teaching License valid?

    Teaching License is valid for a period of 60 months (5 years) for Citizens and 36 months (3 years) for non-citizens.

    How do I renew my license?

    License renewal is done online through the Teacher Registration and Licensing System (TRLS).

    What happens if my license expires?

    An expired license ceases your membership. Practicing with an expired license is an offence and attracts penalties. Teachers are not allowed to practice with an expired license.

    If a Teaching License is not renewed and the Teacher is permanent and pensionable, what happens?

    Employment status (permanent and pensionable) does not exempt one from being licensed.

    How much does license renewal cost?

    Licensing fees are P50 and P120 for Citizens and non-citizens respectively.

    Will I receive a physical or digital license?

    Licenses are issued digitally.

    Do I need to pay annually?

    Yes. The membership subscription is paid annually.

    How do I seek assistance and support for Registration and Licensing?

    Enquiries and queries can be emailed to helpdesk@botepco.org.bw or call 393 3472.

    How can I access the Teacher Registration and Licensing Portal?

    The Portal is accessible at https://trls.gov.bw.

    Teaching Professional Standards

    What are Teaching Professional standards?

    Teaching Professional Standards are statements that promote professional teaching by describing the professional knowledge, competencies, classroom skills and ethics that teachers need to know and be able to do during their professional career to provide learners with knowledge-rich learning opportunities.

    What are Ethical Standards?

    The Code of Ethics sets out BOTEPCO’s ethical guidelines and best practices to follow for honesty, integrity, and professionalism in the teaching profession.

    Continuous Professional Development

    What is Continuous Professional Development (CPD)?

    Continuous Professional Development is a lifelong process through which professionals continuously improve their knowledge, skills, and practices to become more effective in their work. Teachers continually review, renew, and develop their professional knowledge and skills throughout their teaching careers.
