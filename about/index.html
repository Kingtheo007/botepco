    About BOTEPCO

    Learn about our mandate, mission and values.

    Who we are

    The Botswana Teaching Professionals Council (BOTEPCO) is the statutory body established under the Botswana Teaching Professionals Act No. 22 of 2019 to regulate, professionalise and uphold standards within the teaching profession in Botswana. The Council registers and licenses teaching professionals, sets and maintains professional standards, upholds a Code of Ethics, and supports the continuing professional development of teachers across the country. BOTEPCO’s value proposition centres around professionalising teaching in contributing to the ultimate improved quality of education in the Country.

    The Council reports to the Ministry of Child Welfare and Basic Education and is accountable to a Board of Directors.

    Vision and mission

    Our vision

    Building internationally acclaimed teaching professionals.

    Our mission

    To regulate the teaching profession through registration, licensing and professional standards, advocating for teachers to improve the quality of education in Botswana.

    Botswana Teaching Professionals Council Act, No. 22 of 2019

    The mandate of the Council

    The mandate of the Council is to:

    1. Regulate the teaching profession.
    2. Maintain professional and ethical standards for teachers, which include teaching knowledge, skills and competence.
    3. License teachers.
    4. Advise on teacher training issues.
    5. Advocate for the teaching profession.
    6. Maintain a register of teachers.
    7. Establish, publish, review and maintain a Code of Ethics for teachers.
    8. Monitor adherence to the Code of Ethics under paragraph (g).
    9. Review and advise on professional standards for teachers.
    10. Determine the grounds for refusal or withdrawal of registration where necessary.
    11. Determine the criteria and procedures for registration.
    12. Consider and decide on any application for registration.
    13. Promote continuing education, training and professional development of teachers.
    14. Establish panels to investigate and hear complaints against teachers.
    15. Ensure a fair hearing is held in accordance with the procedure determined by the Board.
    16. Conduct research to inform quality teaching.

    Our values

    • Customer-centricity

      We provide responsive, accessible and high-quality regulatory services that create value for teachers, stakeholders and the public.

    • Excellence

      We continuously improve and consistently deliver high-quality services and regulatory outcomes.

    • Agility

      We respond effectively to change and responsibly use technology and innovation to improve regulation and service delivery.

    • Professionalism

      We uphold the highest standards of ethics, competence, integrity and accountability.

    • Transparency

      We act openly, honestly and fairly, and remain accountable for our decisions and actions.

    • Employee-focus

      We foster a respectful, inclusive and empowering workplace that enables our people to grow and perform at their best.
