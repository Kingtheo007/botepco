    The Board

    The Board of Directors provides strategic direction and oversight to the Council. The Board is appointed by the Minister, who considers their diverse skills, experiences and expertise that would aid the Council in fulfilling its mandate. The appointment process also ensures that relevant stakeholders are represented on the Council’s supervisory body.

    • Mr. Geoffrey Mahlaka

      Chairperson

      Read biography of Mr. Geoffrey Mahlaka

      Mr. Geoffrey Mahlaka is a Legal and Risk Management Professional currently and serves as Chairperson of the Council. He holds a Commonwealth Executive MBA from Botswana Open University and an LLB from the University of Botswana and is an admitted Attorney, Conveyancer and Notary Public. He is also a Certified Risk Analyst with additional qualifications in Governance, Risk Management and Compliance, Enterprise Risk Management, Board Governance, and Business Sustainability Management.

      Mr. Mahlaka has extensive experience in legal practice, governance, compliance, insurance, and enterprise risk management. His expertise in corporate governance, legal advisory services and regulatory compliance enables him to provide effective leadership to the Board and support the Council in advancing professionalism and excellence within Botswana's teaching profession.

    • Ms. Maipelo Mighty Kesenye

      Vice Chairperson – Retired School Inspector Constituency

      Read biography of Ms. Maipelo Mighty Kesenye

      Ms. Maipelo Mighty Kesenye is a Retired Senior Education Manager and Educationist in the public education sector and currently serves as Vice Chairperson of the Council. She holds a Bachelor of Philosophy in Educational Studies with a specialisation in Guidance and Counselling, and she also holds an Honorary PhD in Leadership.

      With extensive experience in educational leadership and institutional management, Ms. Kesenye has served in various leadership positions within the Ministry of Higher Education, where she led academic performance improvement initiatives, teacher development programmes, and regional education management activities. Her expertise spans education management, institutional leadership, and the promotion of quality education. Through her experience and commitment to educational excellence, she provides valuable strategic guidance and oversight to the Board.

    Executive Management

    BOTEPCO’s Executive Management Team provides strategic leadership and oversees the implementation of the Council’s mandate to advance teacher professionalisation and uphold professional standards in Botswana.

    • Dr Raphael Dingalo

      Registrar

      Read biography of Dr Raphael Dingalo

      As Registrar, Dr Dingalo leads the Executive Management Team and is responsible for the day-to-day running of the Council, the implementation of the Board’s strategy and the delivery of BOTEPCO’s mandate to register, license and regulate teachers.

    • Njalakangwa Phumaphi

      Director Digital Transformation & Innovation

      Read biography of Njalakangwa Phumaphi

      Njalakangwa Phumaphi leads the Council’s digital transformation, including the Teacher Registration and Licensing System (TRLS), information systems and the use of technology to make BOTEPCO’s services easier for teachers to use.

    • Mpho Leteane

      Director Legal Service & Board Secretary

      Read biography of Mpho Leteane

      Mpho Leteane provides legal advice to the Council, oversees regulatory and disciplinary legal matters, and serves as Secretary to the Board, supporting good governance and compliance with the Act.

      Read biography of Ntsholeng Thabana
