    More questions and answers
    What is BOTEPCO and what is its mandate?

    The Botswana Teaching Professionals Council (BOTEPCO) is a regulatory body under the Ministry of Child Welfare & Basic Education, established by the Botswana Teaching Professionals Council Act, No. 22 of 2019. BOTEPCO’s mandate is to:

    1. regulate the teaching profession;
    2. maintain professional and ethical standards for teachers, which include teaching knowledge, skills and competence;
    3. license teachers;
    4. advise on teacher training issues;
    5. advocate for the teaching profession;
    6. maintain a register of teachers;
    7. establish, publish, review and maintain a Code of Ethics for teachers;
    8. monitor adherence to the Code of Ethics under paragraph (g);
    9. review and advise on professional standards of teachers;
    10. determine the grounds for refusal or withdrawal of registration where necessary;
    11. determine the criteria and procedures for registration;
    12. consider and decide on any application for registration;
    13. promote continuing education, training and professional development of teachers;
    14. establish panels to investigate and hear complaints against teachers;
    15. ensure a fair hearing is held in accordance with the procedure determined by the Board; and
    16. conduct research to inform quality teaching.
    How do I register on the BOTEPCO system?

    Registration is done online through the Teacher Registration and Licensing System (TRLS) portal, which you can use from anywhere. The live system is at trls.gov.bw, and you create your account at trls.gov.bw/customer/signup. Account creation is a multi-step process; the sign-up form runs to six steps.

    The process works as follows:

    1. Visit the official TRLS portal: https://trls.gov.bw/customer/signup.
    2. Create an account using a valid email address, a username and a secure password.
    3. Log in to the system and complete the electronic application form.
    4. Provide the required personal, professional and qualification information and upload the supporting documents requested by the system.
    5. Submit your application for review and verification by BOTEPCO.
    6. Monitor your email and the TRLS portal for notifications about your application status.
    7. If your application is approved, follow the instructions provided to pay the applicable registration and licensing fees within the stipulated period.

    Applicants should ensure that all information is accurate and all required documents are valid before submitting their applications. Incomplete applications may be rejected.

    What are the requirements for registration and licensing?
    • Certified soft copies of relevant educational qualifications
    • Certified soft copies of valid National Identity Card/Omang (citizens)
    • Certified soft copies of Passport Bio-Page (non-citizens)
    • Certified soft copy of marriage certificate or sworn affidavit as proof of change of names
    • Soft copies of the Botswana Qualifications Authority (BQA) letter of verification of qualifications obtained from learning institutions outside Botswana
    • Visa card for payment
    How do I pay the registration and licensing fees?

    Registration and licensing fees are payable through the TRLS process after BOTEPCO has approved the application. Applicants should:

    1. Log in to their TRLS account.
    2. Check the application status and approval notification.
    3. Follow the payment instructions provided by the system.
    4. Complete payment.
    5. Retain the payment confirmation or receipt for future reference.
    6. Download your licence from the portal.

    Annual subscription fees: Citizens P50 · Non-citizens P120

    The formal licensing cycle operates on an annual membership timeline running from 1 January to 31 December. Standard annual renewal payments must be processed before the start of the next cycle, guided by the three-month advance notification window.

    How long does it take for an application to be fully processed and endorsed?

    The time required to process an application depends on the completeness and accuracy of the information submitted, the validity of the supporting documents, and the time required for verification and assessment by BOTEPCO.

    Applicants are encouraged to submit complete and accurate applications and respond promptly to any requests for additional information or documentation. Notifications regarding application progress and outcomes are communicated electronically through the TRLS system.
